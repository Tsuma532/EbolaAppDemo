When to seek help

If you travelled from or were exposed in an area with an Ebola outbreak and develop fever, unusual weakness, headache, muscle pain, vomiting or diarrhoea, contact local health authorities immediately.

Do not self-diagnose
Important public-health noteSymptoms may appear 2–21 days after infection. Monitoring is based on possible exposure and official guidance—not simply on the length of a foreign stay. A symptom questionnaire cannot diagnose Ebola.

Suggested safeguards before deployment

  • Written approval and clear legal authority from relevant public health and border agencies.
  • Collect only information necessary for a defined public-health purpose.
  • Role-based staff access, audit logs, encryption, retention limits and deletion procedures.
  • Human review for alerts; no automatic denial of travel or services based only on a form.
  • Accessible multilingual design and a non-digital alternative at border points.
  • Security testing, incident response and independent privacy review.
